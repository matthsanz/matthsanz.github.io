Support

One address, and a person on the other end.

rowsmith.support@gmail.com

Questions, defects or requests all go to the same address. Expect a reply within 24–48 hours.

What to send with it

Sending these four saves a whole round trip.

  • Your Unity version The full number, such as 6000.0.66f1.
  • Your Rowsmith version It is in the package documentation.
  • What happened What you did, what happened, and what you expected instead.
  • The error message Its text, if there is one — or a screenshot.

It may already be answered

The documentation inside the package has a Known limits section, and it covers the most likely questions — the import that never creates new assets, the sub-assets that are not listed, and what happens to Ctrl+Z after a recompile.

The short version is on the Rowsmith page →
